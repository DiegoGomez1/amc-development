Our Story

A Legacy Built on
Integrity & Vision

Since 2014, AMC Development has been turning underdeveloped land and opportunity into lasting value across South Florida.

Our Mission

Building Communities,
Creating Value

AMC Development LLC was founded with a singular focus: to identify opportunities in South Florida's real estate market and develop them with quality craftsmanship, strategic vision, and respect for the communities we build in.

Over a decade, we've evolved from a custom home builder into a diversified real estate investment company—managing a growing portfolio of income-producing commercial properties while simultaneously developing some of the region's most significant affordable housing and multi-family projects.

Our pipeline includes a 43-unit apartment community and a 248-unit affordable housing development—projects that reflect our commitment to addressing South Florida's housing needs at scale.
