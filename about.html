Our Story

A legacy built on integrity and vision.

Since 2014, AMC Development has turned underused land into lasting value across South Florida, one carefully chosen site at a time.

Mission

Building communities, creating value.

AMC was founded with a single focus: find the right opportunities in South Florida real estate and develop them with quality craftsmanship, strategic vision and respect for the neighborhoods we build in.

Over a decade the firm has grown from a custom home builder into a diversified developer and owner. It holds income-producing commercial and residential property while bringing some of its most significant work, a 43-unit apartment community and a 248-unit affordable housing development, through entitlement.
